Mfano wa Matumizi

curl -X POST https://yourdomain.com/api/v1/chat \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-API-KEY: sb_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx" \
  -d '{"message": "Habari, unaweza kunisaidiaje?"}'

Jibu litarudi kama {"reply": "..."}. Weka historia ya mazungumzo kwenye field ya hiari history (orodha ya {role, content}) endapo unataka muktadha wa mazungumzo yaliyopita.
